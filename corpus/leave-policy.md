# Leave Policy

## Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued at 1.5 days per month. Up to 10 unused days can be carried forward to the next year. Any remaining unused days lapse on 31 March.

## Sick leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days. Unused sick leave does not carry forward.

## Parental leave
The primary caregiver receives 26 weeks of paid parental leave. The secondary caregiver receives 4 weeks of paid parental leave, to be taken within 6 months of the birth or adoption.

## Bereavement leave
Employees may take up to 5 days of paid bereavement leave after the loss of an immediate family member.

## Applying for leave
Apply for leave in PeopleHub. For planned leave of more than 3 days, apply at least 2 weeks in advance. Managers approve or decline leave requests within 3 working days.
