# IT Security Policy

## Passwords and multi-factor authentication
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, the VPN, and PeopleHub. Never share your password with anyone. The IT team will never ask for your password by email, chat, or phone.

## Phishing
If you receive a suspicious email, do not click its links or open its attachments. Report it to report-phish@northwind.example and then delete it. If you clicked a link or entered your password, contact IT immediately at extension 4357.

## Laptops and devices
Company laptops must use full-disk encryption and install security updates within 7 days of release. Report a lost or stolen device to IT within 24 hours.

## Data classification
Northwind Labs classifies data as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted. Restricted data, such as salary information and personal records, may only be shared with authorised HR staff.
