# Remote Work Policy

## Hybrid schedule
Employees work from the office at least 3 days per week. Each team agrees on its shared office days.

## Home office stipend
Employees receive a one-time home office stipend of ₹15,000 for furniture and equipment. The stipend can be claimed within the first 3 months after joining.

## Security when working remotely
When working remotely, connect through the company VPN. Do not use public or shared computers for work.

## Working from another country
Employees may work from another country for up to 30 days per year with prior approval from their manager and HR.
