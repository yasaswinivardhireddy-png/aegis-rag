# HR Casework Procedure

## Opening a case
HR business partners open a case in CaseTrack within 1 working day of receiving a complaint. Each case receives an ID in the format HR-CASE-YYYY-NNNN.

## Investigation timeline
Investigations should conclude within 30 days. Interview notes are stored only in CaseTrack and never in email.
