# Employee Handbook

## About Northwind Labs
Northwind Labs builds analytics software for logistics companies. Our headquarters is in Bengaluru, and we have a second office in Pune.

## Working hours
Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 16:00 IST, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours, employees may arrange their schedule with their manager. The standard work week is 40 hours.

## Probation and notice period
New employees complete a probation period of 6 months. During probation, the notice period is 15 days. After confirmation, the notice period is 60 days.

## Code of conduct
We expect respectful and inclusive behaviour from everyone. Harassment or discrimination of any kind is not tolerated. Report concerns to hr-help@northwind.example or through the anonymous EthicsLine.

## Getting help
For HR questions, email hr-help@northwind.example. For IT support, email it-help@northwind.example or call extension 4357. For payroll questions, email payroll@northwind.example.
