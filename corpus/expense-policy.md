# Travel and Expense Policy

## Submitting expense claims
Submit expense claims in PeopleHub within 30 days of the expense, with itemised receipts. Approved claims are reimbursed in the next payroll cycle.

## Flights and hotels
Book economy class for flights shorter than 6 hours. A director may approve business class for flights longer than 6 hours. Hotel stays are reimbursed up to ₹8,000 per night in metro cities.

## Meals
Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to ₹1,500 per day. Alcohol is not reimbursed.

## Approvals
Any expense above ₹25,000 requires approval from your department head before booking.
