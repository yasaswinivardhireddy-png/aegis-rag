# CloudPrint Printer FAQ

## Printing from your laptop
Install the CloudPrint client from the Software Center, then select the printer named NW-Floor2. Print jobs are released when you tap your badge on the printer.

## Paper jams
Open the front panel, gently remove the jammed paper, and close the panel. If the error continues, restart the printer by holding the power button for 10 seconds.

## Error E42
Error E42 means the print queue is full. Wait 5 minutes and try again. If the error continues, contact IT at extension 4357.

## Sign-in problems
If the CloudPrint client keeps asking you to sign in, sign out of the client, restart your laptop, and sign in again with your company account.
