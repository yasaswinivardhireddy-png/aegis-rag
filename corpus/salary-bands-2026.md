# Salary Bands 2026

## Classification
Restricted. Share only with authorised HR staff.

## Engineering salary bands
Annual base salary ranges in INR for engineering roles:
- L1 Associate Engineer: ₹6,20,000 to ₹8,90,000
- L2 Software Engineer: ₹9,40,000 to ₹14,20,000
- L3 Senior Software Engineer: ₹16,80,000 to ₹24,60,000
- L4 Staff Engineer: ₹27,30,000 to ₹36,90,000
- L5 Principal Engineer: ₹38,50,000 to ₹52,00,000

## Annual review
Salary adjustments take effect on 1 April. The 2026 merit budget is 7.5% of base payroll.
